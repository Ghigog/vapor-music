# Support

**Where to send a problem:** `<CONTACT ADDRESS — Dylan to fill in>`

Vapor Music has no telemetry and no crash reporting, deliberately — see
[PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md). Nothing is sent when the app misbehaves. That is the
point, and it has one consequence: **if you do not tell us, nobody knows.** A
fault you hit and shrug off is a fault that stays in the app.

---

## What to include

Five things. The first two are the ones that make a report actionable, and the
first is the one people leave out.

1. **The build.** Open **Settings** and scroll to the Vapor Music name at the
   bottom; underneath it is a line like `2.0.0 · a3f9c21`. It is selectable —
   copy it. Without it a report cannot be tied to a particular version of the
   code, and "it crashed" and "it crashed in this build" are very different
   messages.
2. **What you did, in order.** The three or four steps before it went wrong.
   "Pressed Analyse, switched to the Albums tab while it ran, pressed a track"
   is worth more than a paragraph describing the result.
3. **What you expected, and what happened instead.**
4. **Your platform.** macOS, Windows, Linux or Android, and which version.
5. **Whether it happens every time.** Once, sometimes, or always.

## Useful, when they apply

- **A screenshot or a screen recording.** Especially for anything about
  layout, artwork, or a control that will not respond.
- **The track,** if one track does it and others do not — its format, its
  length, and where it came from (a local folder, or your WebDAV server).
- **Whether the app was talking to anything.** Was it mid-analysis? Syncing
  with another device? Were lyric and artwork lookups on?
- **Console output,** if you are comfortable getting it. Desktop builds print
  errors to standard output, so running the binary from a terminal will show
  them.

## Before you send

- **Nothing is collected automatically, so nothing is sent without you.**
  Anything you attach — a screenshot, a path, a log — is something you chose to
  include. Screenshots of the Library and paths on your machine will show your
  folder names and file names; if that matters to you, crop or redact them.
- **Never send your WebDAV password**, and nobody will ever ask you for it.
  Naming your provider ("Koofr", "a Nextcloud on my own box") is useful; the
  credentials are not.

## What to expect

Vapor Music is written by one person. There is no support desk and no
guaranteed response time. Reports are read.
