How Daylight says "yours"
One green, one meaning. #2E9E6B never marks a button or a success toast — only "this is local, this is yours". Library header, Now Playing metadata, the analysis progress, the Your Data card. Four places, one idea, impossible to misread.
Trust is a screen, not a paragraph. Your Data shows the actual paths, actual sizes, an Export everything button, and a plain list of what Vapor never does. Sovereignty you can audit beats sovereignty you're told about.
Daylight, not a dark room. A sky gradient at the top, warm paper at the bottom, and a horizon in between. Frutiger Aero's optimism comes from the light and the soft blooms — not from gel buttons or bubbles.
Copy that isn't afraid. No padlocks, no shields, no "military-grade". "On this device." "No account." "Listening…" Confidence, not defensiveness.
Art is the only saturated thing. On warm white, album covers carry all the colour — which is exactly what the design doc asks for and what dark mode quietly prevented.
The mark, generated
idle

blending
No PNG. The mark is a damped, chirped cosine sampled into a variable-width ribbon, then shaded in four passes: iridescent gradient along the path, screen-space volume, a travelling hotspot, and a specular core riding the lit edge.
y = −A·e^(−dt) · cos(2π(f₀t + ct²) + φ)
w = W(0.34 + 0.66·e^(−1.25t))
w ≤ 0.84·R  ← curvature clamp
Every parameter is animatable, so idle breathes, thinking tightens its chirp, blending swirls the hotspot, and playing rides real track energy. It ports straight across: the sampler is the same function, the outline becomes draw_colored_polygon(), and the four shading passes become one fragment shader over the ribbon's UV.
