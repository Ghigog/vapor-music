What the recreation exposes
· A 44px row with 32px art gives the library ~15 rows per screen — a lot of small items, none of them the hero. Album art is decoration at that size, not navigation.
· The bottom bar merges navigation and transport into eight 44px controls in 374px. Every one is ~42px wide with an 11px clipped label. Play is the same size and weight as Settings.
· There is no Now Playing screen at all on mobile — the desktop sidebar held that, and narrow layout drops the sidebar.
· The Vibe workbench keeps its desktop information density: five metadata rows per card, in 11px type.
