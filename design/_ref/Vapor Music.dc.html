What changed, and why
Eight buttons became three. Library · Vibe · Settings in a floating glass pill. Search moved into Library, Playlists became a filter chip, Queue became a sheet. Transport lives with the music, not with navigation.
The library is browsed by artwork. Two-column tiles at 163px instead of 15 rows at 44px — six albums per screen you can recognise at a glance. Songs view keeps a list, but at 66px with 48px art.
Play is 84px and glowing. Prev/next are 52px and quiet. One thing is obviously the primary action.
The AI DJ has a room. On Now Playing it is a one-line chip: what's next, when, how. Tap it for the workbench — overlapping waveforms, phase lock, alternates. The DJ toggle is the progressive-disclosure switch for everything technical.
Glass earns its keep. Only floating layers are glass: pill, mini player, sheets, blend card. Content sits on the void so text always clears 4.5:1.
